Civics 2025 — Privacy policy

Last updated: 27 August 2026. This page exists so Google Play has a policy URL to link. The full, authoritative policy is the About & privacy screen inside the app (Home → About & privacy), which stays current with each app version.

Summary: Civics 2025 is an independent, unofficial practice tool for the USCIS civics test. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or approved by USCIS or any U.S. government agency.

The developer does not operate a server for this app and does not collect, sell, or share your information. Practice answers, scores, streaks, settings, and an optionally downloaded study-guide PDF are stored only in the app's private storage on your device, are not backed up by Android, and are deleted when you uninstall the app. The app has no account, sign-in, advertising identifier, analytics, or crash-reporting SDK. Tapping to download the study guide sends a request to uscis.gov, not to the developer.

For full details — including notifications, permissions, backups, and how to delete data — open the app and go to About & privacy.

Questions about this policy

There is no web form. If you obtained the app from Google Play, use the Play Store listing's email contact for the developer.
